Welcome

A Real Farm. Real Deer. Real Hunts.

Hunters' Den is a family-run hunting ground on our working cattle farm in the Manawatu. Red deer and fallow herds roam our paddocks and bush gullies — and they eat more of our pasture than we do. We turned that problem into your adventure.

  • Every skill level — first-timers, weekend hunters and trophy experts all welcome
  • One-on-one guiding — you hunt with an experienced local guide, never in a crowd
  • Fair chase, ethical harvest — clean shots, full use of the animal, nothing wasted
  • Close to town — an easy drive from Palmerston North, with farm-stay accommodation

Safety & Training

Safety Is Non-Negotiable

Every hunt starts with a firearms safety briefing, no exceptions. Beginners spend time on our private range before walking up. We carry first-aid kits, radios and personal locator beacons on every trip, and we hunt within your ability — fitness, experience and weather all shape the plan for the day.
